PixNative Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-12-01

PixNative ("the app") is a photo editing, beautification, motion and greeting card app for Android. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

Summary

  • The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.
  • The app does not have internet access (it does not request the Android INTERNET permission).
  • There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • The optional PixNative Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (see "Purchases").

Photos and videos

  • You choose photos with the Android photo picker. If you turn on Photos on Home, the app asks for permission to read your photos so it can suggest your best ones on the home screen. To do this it looks at your photos on your device (for example, how many faces they have and how sharp they are) and keeps that small index in its private storage; nothing is uploaded.
  • All processing (face and person detection, segmentation, beautification, effects, animation and video creation) happens on your device using models bundled with the app.
  • Your photos are never uploaded. Results are saved to your device's gallery (in the "PixNative" album) only when you tap Save.
  • You can turn off Photos on Home in Settings, or revoke the permission in Android settings at any time.

Data stored on your device

The app keeps your projects, preferences (such as language) and small preview images in its private storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app hands the image or video to the app you choose (for example Instagram or WhatsApp) using standard Android sharing. That app's own privacy policy then applies. PixNative does not send anything anywhere by itself.

If you choose to send a diagnostic log from Settings, the log text is passed to the app you pick (for example email). It contains technical information only, not your photos.

Purchases

PixNative Pro is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment and your payment details under Google's privacy policy; PixNative never sees them. The app only asks Google Play on your device whether Pro has been bought and stores a "Pro is active" flag in its private storage. No purchase information is sent to us.

Downloads

Some optional AI features are delivered as additional content by Google Play (Play Asset Delivery). Google Play handles this download; PixNative does not contact any server.

App updates

The app may ask Google Play on your device whether a newer version is available and, if so, offer to install it. Google Play handles the check and the download; PixNative does not contact any server.

Notifications

The app shows notifications only to report the progress of video renders and photo fixes you started.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date.
